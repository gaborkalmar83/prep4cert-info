Prep 4 Cert - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Prep 4 Cert is an Android app with practice questions for certification exams, published by Kalmarium Services BV (the Netherlands). This policy explains what the app does with data.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or share personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your study data stays on your device: answers, mistake counters, saved questions, tags, test results, settings, and a record of which exams you unlocked. It is not sent to us. If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup, under Google's terms. Uninstalling the app deletes the data from the device.

Purchases

Exams and the exam bundle are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and holds your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which products were bought. We do not receive your name, email address or card details.

Links to other sites

Answers link to official documentation, for example on learn.microsoft.com. These pages open in your browser and are covered by the privacy policies of their owners.

Permissions

The app requests no dangerous permissions. It uses the Google Play Billing permission to offer purchases.

AI transparency

The practice questions and explanations are drafted with AI assistance, checked against the vendor's official documentation and reviewed by the author, a Microsoft Certified Trainer. The app itself does not run an AI system on your device or send your input to one.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for professional certifications and is not directed at children.

Independence

Prep 4 Cert is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Microsoft or The Open Group. The questions are original practice questions and are not taken from real exams.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: kalmariumservices@gmail.com.
